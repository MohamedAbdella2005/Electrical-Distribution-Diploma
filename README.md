# ⚡ Electrical Distribution Design Project

A comprehensive, real-world electrical distribution design simulation completed as part of the **Electrical Distribution Diploma** organized by **EPE Group**, under the mentorship of **Eng. Mohamed Emam** and **Eng. Mahmoud Elzeiny**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project covers the complete design lifecycle of an electrical distribution system for a commercial/industrial building, adhering to international standards (IEC, NEC) and local codes. The design spans everything from medium-voltage incoming points down to final branch circuits.

---

## 🛠 Scope of Work & Engineering Stages

### 1. Load Estimation & Power Sources
- Estimated initial total project electrical demand using standard area load densities (VA/m²) and appropriate diversity/demand factors.
- Sized main power transformers, backup diesel generators, and the incoming substation configuration to ensure continuous power reliability.

### 2. Lighting Design (Indoor & Outdoor)
- Performed lux level calculations ensuring standard illuminance for different functional areas.
- Selection of luminaires, branch circuiting, switching schemes, and emergency exit lighting integration.
- Designed outdoor and street lighting networks with layout optimization and feeder pillar coordination.

### 3. Small Power & Socket Distribution
- Distributed normal, emergency, and clean UPS socket outlets based on architectural layouts.
- Circuit protection sizing and protective earthing with residual current devices (RCDs) for safety.

### 4. HVAC & Mechanical Equipment Feeder Sizing
- Sized feeder cables, circuit breakers, and local isolator switches for heavy mechanical loads (AHUs, Chillers, Pumps, and Elevators), taking into account starting currents and derating factors.

### 5. Panel Boards & Single Line Diagram (SLD)
- Designed Main Distribution Boards (MDB), Sub-Distribution Boards (SMDB), and Final DB schedules with balanced phase loading.
- Developed the complete **Single Line Diagram (SLD)** illustrating the hierarchy from MV/LV switchgear and ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch) down to end loads.

### 6. Technical Calculations & BOQ
- **Voltage Drop:** Verified that cumulative voltage drops on all feeders fall strictly within permissible code limits.
- **Short Circuit Analysis:** Calculated fault levels at various distribution tiers to determine the required breaking capacity (kA) and selectivity for circuit breakers.
- **Bill of Quantities (BOQ):** Prepared an engineering quantity take-off for cables, containment systems, switchgear, and fixtures.
